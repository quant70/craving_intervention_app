The Dual Processor Problem

Your brain operates on two distinct systems. The Lizard Brain (System 1) is the ancient, automatic processor responsible for survival (Fight/Flight) and habits. It is fast, effortless, and powerful. The Neocortex (System 2) is your rational, conscious mind. It is slow, effortful, and easily exhausted.

"The Lizard Brain's primal drives form the foundation of System 1's speed. When a craving is triggered, it is System 1 that initiates the response before you can even think."

Survival vs. Thriving

When the Amygdala (Lizard Brain) detects a threat or a craving trigger, it hijacks the system. Logic shuts down, and survival instincts take over.

Techniques like EFT and Mindfulness are designed to shift this physiological state, moving you from the red zone (Survival) to the blue zone (Regulation).

Rewiring Protocols

A. EFT Tapping: The Amygdala Override

Tapping sends a calming signal to the amygdala while you focus on the stress. This disconnects the "Panic" signal from the "Trigger."

Somatic Interruption

EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique) targets the hardware directly. By tapping on meridian points, you send safety signals to the body.

  • The Setup: "Even though I have this craving, I accept myself." (Reduces shame/resistance).
  • The Signal: Disarms the Fight/Flight response in the Amygdala.
  • The Result: The trigger exists, but the physical compulsion fades.

Reward Value Update (RVU)

You cannot fight a habit with willpower. You must disenchant it. By paying close attention (Curiosity), you realize the old reward isn't actually that good.

Negative Prediction Error: When the brain expects a "10/10" reward (Dopamine hit) but actually experiences a "3/10" reality (Stomach ache, regret), the habit updates and weakens.
