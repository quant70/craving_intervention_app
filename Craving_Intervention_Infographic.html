Willpower is a limited resource. To alleviate cravings, we must bypass the struggle and update the brain's internal Reward Value using curiosity and reinforcement learning.

Phase 1: The Map

Before we can change a habit, we must make the non-conscious conscious. Every craving follows a specific loop. Understanding this structure is the first step to dismantling it.

1. The Cue

The trigger. Not the substance, but the feeling or context preceding it.

2. The Routine

The automatic behavior. The craving itself is just a physical sensation.

3. The Reward

The outcome. Usually temporary relief, distraction, or numbing.

Identify Your Trigger Category

The true trigger is rarely the object of desire. It is usually an internal state or external context. Recognizing the category is actionable insight.

  • Environmental: Location and sight (e.g., passing a bakery).
  • Emotional/Internal: Stress, boredom, or fatigue seeking relief.
  • Social/Temporal: Routine habits (e.g., lunchtime, watching TV).

Phase 2: The Hack

We must teach the Orbitofrontal Cortex that the old habit is unrewarding. This creates a Negative Prediction Error—where the expected high reward fails to materialize.

Reward Value Update

The brain chases dopamine. If you pay close attention to the indulgence (mindful eating/using), you realize the actual reward is low.

The Disenchantment Practice

"Focus entirely on the taste, texture, and aftermath. Does the reward diminish rapidly? Observe the regret immediately after."

The Disenchantment Curve

Expected vs. Actual Reward over mindful repetitions.

Phase 3: The Rewire

The brain needs a "Bigger, Better Offer." We replace the old routine with Curiosity and the reward with Mastery.

The Old Loop

Trigger Stress / Anxiety
Routine Eat / Consume
Reward Temporary Numbness

The New Loop

Trigger Stress / Anxiety
Routine Curiosity & Investigation
Reward Self-Mastery & Clarity

Protocol: The 5-Minute Curiosity Block

When the cue hits, substitute the habit immediately.

Minute 0-1

Inhale & Exhale

"I recognize the craving. I am allowing the sensation to be here."

Minute 1-4

Deep Investigation

Observe physical location and movement. Is it heat? Tension? Buzzing?

Minute 5

Future Visualization

Connect the victory to your future self. "I chose freedom over automatism."
